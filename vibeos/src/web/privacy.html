VibeOS · Privacy

Local-first by design

VibeOS stores its core workspaces, memories, flows, settings and activity locally on your device or in the browser profile running VibeOS.

The Android app can optionally read notification context and the foreground application package name only after you explicitly grant Android system access. That context is stored in app-local storage and is not uploaded by VibeOS v1.0.0.

VibeOS does not claim cloud synchronization, end-to-end encryption, or external account access unless a future connector is explicitly enabled.

Export and share actions occur only when you invoke them.
